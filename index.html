The Xiakra Social System sets how the agent console looks and sounds on every social platform.

One grid, one typeface, one signal colour and two brand graphics. Every post, carousel and reel starts from a template, so the feed reads as one product: precise, calm and built for operators.

The Xiakra story

The mark
An X built from four circuit traces, each ending in a node. Agents, connected. Every brand graphic is cut from it.
One idea per frame
The headline states it, the visual proves it, the body adds one fact or nothing.
Dark console, one signal
Void is the ground. Crimson marks the single thing to look at, never more than 2 % of a frame.
Show the product
Content frames show real console UI. The Core, the Trace and the Network frame it; they never replace it.
01

formats & grid

Three artboards cover every platform. Four fixed zones, a 72 px margin and one alignment rule hold every post together.

//01 formats & platforms Design at 1080 wide · export stills at 2×
1080 × 1350 · 4:5Feed & carouselInstagram feed + carousel, Facebook, LinkedIn image and PDF carousel, Threads. The default.
1080 × 1080 · 1:1SquareX, LinkedIn single image, Facebook, square ads.
1080 × 1920 · 9:16Reel & storyReels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn vertical video.

Export

  • Stills: PNG-24, sRGB, 2× (2160 wide), no transparency.
  • LinkedIn carousel: 4:5 slides as one PDF, in order.
  • Video: MP4 H.264 High, yuv420p, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, CRF 16.
  • Audio: AAC 48 kHz 320 kbps, −14 LUFS, peak −1 dBTP.
  • Never resize one format into another. Open its template.
//01 grid & zones 72 margin · 6 columns · 24 gutter · 8 baseline
Zones
Z1 logo + counter. Z2 eyebrow, headline, body. Z3 one proof visual. Z4 series label, swipe cue, progress. Text lives only in Z2.
Alignment
Flush left on x 72. The closing CTA frame is the only centred layout. Never right-align or justify.
Spacing
8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 only. Eyebrow → headline → body 32 apart. Card radius 28, padding 40–48.

05

graphic elements

The Trace, the Core and the Network. All three come from the mark and the console, so the feed is recognisable before anyone reads a word.

//05 the Trace Wordless brand graphic
01 · the markFour circuit traces, 45° legs, each ending in a node.
02 · one armStroke 1.36, node ⌀ 4.84. Node = 3.56 × stroke.
03 · the TraceThe arm, scaled up and bled off an edge, plus a thin companion line.
Geometry
45° legs only, one sharp elbow. Every visible end is a node. Stroke 24–36 on 1080. Companion: 6 % of stroke, offset 1.9 ×, Rose 50 %.
Colour
Signal fill on Void. Ivory or Void on Oxblood. Oxblood on Bone. Never Rose as the fill.
Placement
One per frame. Enters from an edge, elbow points in. Never crosses Z2 text; node ≥ 40 px clear of Z4. Not on dense UI slides.
//05 the Core & the Network From the Xiakra cover and login

The Core

A sphere of connected agents: 240 nodes, 3 links each, depth-faded, 10 lit agents. Hero of covers, CTAs, the reel hook and the end card. One per frame. Radius 380 on 4:5, 300 on 1:1, 400 on 9:16, 150 on CTA. In motion it turns 0.18 rad/s, never faster. Chips may pin to it; body text may not sit on it.

The Network

The approved login background. Lines Rose 16 % at 1.25 px, nodes Rose 42 %, about 70 nodes per 4:5 frame, max 3 lit agents. Drawn in Void on Oxblood colour-block frames. Darkest behind Z2, brightest behind Z3.

06

social templates

Six carousel slides, three formats and three reels. Swap the words and the proof; keep everything else.

//06 carousel 4:5 · 6 slides · Agent Studio 101
Sequence
Hook → mechanism → colour block → detail → proof → CTA. 3–10 slides allowed; 6 is standard.
Consistency
One series label, one dark ground family, one Oxblood block. Counter and progress always match the slide count.
Rhythm
Brand graphics on slides 1, 3, 5 and 6. Slides 2 and 4 stay clean so the UI reads.
